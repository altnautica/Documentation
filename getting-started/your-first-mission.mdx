<Warning>
Read [Safety](/operations/safety) before flying a mission. An uploaded mission flies without stick input once the drone is in Auto, so set a geofence and know how to take control back.
</Warning>

This guide plans a survey grid over an area, uploads it to the flight controller and flies it in Auto. Fly [Your first flight](/getting-started/your-first-flight) first so you know the Cockpit controls.

## Before you start

- Mission Control open with the drone in the fleet. You can plan with no drone connected and upload later.
- A drone with a 3D GPS fix and a charged battery.
- An open outdoor area.

## Plan the survey

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Plan">
    Click **Plan** in the top navigation. With no plan open, the **FLIGHT PLANS** library sits at the left of the map. Click **New Plan**. The library hides and the planner opens: the planner bar on top, the plan outline on the left, the tool rail on the map, the inspector on the right and the review dock along the bottom of the map.

    <Frame caption="Plan view with an empty mission.">
      ![Mission Control Plan view with a new empty plan: planner bar, plan outline, tool rail and the Mission inspector](/images/mission-control/plan-empty.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set the mission defaults">
    Click the plan name in the planner bar and type a name. With nothing selected, the inspector shows **Mission**; in **Defaults**, set **Altitude** and **Speed**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Draw the survey area">
    Press `G` to open the pattern settings, pick **Survey grid**, then click **Draw area** and click the map to place the corners of the area. You can also draw first with the polygon tool (`P`) on the tool rail. Make the area slightly larger than you need, so the image footprints overlap at the edges.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Generate the pattern">
    Choose a **Survey preset** or **Camera profile**, or a deliverable preset (**Orthomosaic**, **3D model**, **Fast / low detail**). Set **Altitude above home**, **Line spacing** and **Speed**. **Show Advanced** adds **Grid angle**, **Trigger distance**, wind alignment and more.

    Click **Generate**. **Pattern stats** shows the distance, estimated time, waypoints and photos. Click **Apply N waypoints to mission** to put the pattern into the mission. Applying replaces every waypoint already in the mission. Add a Return to Launch or Land waypoint at the end so the drone comes home when the survey finishes.

    <Frame caption="Survey grid generated over a polygon.">
      ![Mission Control Plan view with a survey grid of parallel legs over a drawn polygon](/images/mission-control/plan-survey-pattern.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add a geofence and rally points">
    Click **Geofence** in the plan outline, draw a boundary with a margin around the survey area and set its breach action. Mark safe alternate landing spots with the rally point tool (`Y`). See [Geofences and rally points](/mission-control/geofences-rally).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check the mission">
    The **Issues** tab in the dock (`I`) checks the mission continuously as you edit. Fix every error it lists; sending stays blocked until there are none. Warnings are advisory. The **Terrain** tab shows the altitude along the route against the ground.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To try the plan without flying, open **Simulate** in the top navigation. See [Simulation](/mission-control/simulation).

## Send to the drone

1. Connect the drone and pick it in **Vehicle** in the planner bar.
2. Click **Send to vehicle**, check the mission, geofence and rally points are ticked, then click **Send**. Each item reports **Sent** or **Failed**.
3. Choose **⋯ › Download from drone** and compare the result with your plan. The waypoints, altitudes and commands should match.

## Fly the mission

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pre-flight checks">
    Run the same checks as in [Your first flight](/getting-started/your-first-flight): GPS fix, battery, level attitude, pre-flight checklist, wind.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Arm and take off">
    Open the drone's **Cockpit** tab. Use **Arm** (slide to confirm), then **Take Off** (hold to confirm, with the take-off altitude shown in the sheet), or take off by hand in **Alt Hold** or **Loiter**. Check the drone is stable at a few metres.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Switch to Auto">
    Use **Auto**. It is in the skill drawer; add it to an empty skill-bar slot before the flight. The drone flies to the first waypoint and works through the survey, then runs the finish action.

    <Frame caption="A mission running in Auto on the Flight tab.">
      ![Flight tab of a drone flying its mission in Auto, with the route on the map and the active waypoint marked](/images/mission-control/mission-executing.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Watch and take control if needed">
    Watch altitude, ground speed and battery. To stop the mission:

    - **Pause** pauses the mission and holds the drone in place.
    - **Loiter** holds position.
    - **Return to Home** flies back to the launch point and lands.
    - **Land** lands where the drone is.

    **Kill Motors** stops the motors in the air and the drone falls. Use it only in an emergency.
  </Step>
</Steps>
