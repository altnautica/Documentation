<Warning>
Read [Safety](/operations/safety) and set your failsafes before your first flight. Arming spins the propellers. Check the rules for where you fly on [Regulatory](/operations/regulatory).
</Warning>

This guide takes a built and calibrated drone from the desk to a short manual hover and back, using the **Cockpit** tab in Mission Control.

## Before you start

- A drone with an ArduPilot, PX4, Betaflight or iNav flight controller, props secured and a charged battery.
- Mission Control open and the drone in the fleet. See [Quickstart: Mission Control](/getting-started/quickstart-mission-control) for USB, or [Quickstart: Drone Agent](/getting-started/quickstart-drone-agent) for a companion computer.
- A gamepad that the browser's Gamepad API can read, or an RC transmitter bound to the flight controller.
- An open area away from people, buildings and restricted airspace.

A drone that has never flown needs its accelerometer, compass and RC calibrated first: open the drone, then **Setup** → **Calibration**. See [Calibration](/mission-control/calibration).

## Fly

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the drone">
    Click the drone in the **Fleet** sidebar. The node header shows its name, link state and tabs. On the **Cockpit** tab, the safety band below it shows the armed state, mode, battery, GPS and link.

    <Frame caption="Node header and Cockpit safety band for a connected drone.">
      ![Node header with the drone's tabs above the Cockpit safety band showing armed state, mode, battery, GPS and link](/images/mission-control/node-header.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Run pre-flight checks">
    On the **Flight** tab, confirm:

    - GPS has a 3D fix or better, if you plan to use Loiter or Return to Home.
    - Battery voltage matches a full charge.
    - The attitude indicator is level with the drone on flat ground.
    - RC channels move with your sticks, if you use an RC transmitter.
    - The flight mode is **Stabilize** or **Alt Hold**.

    Work through the **Pre-flight checklist** in the **Cockpit** tab. Fix any flight controller pre-arm failure before arming.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set up the gamepad">
    Connect the gamepad, then bind and calibrate it in **Settings** → **Input Devices**. In the **Cockpit** tab, turn the **STICK** chip to **ON**.

    Mission Control sends stick input to the flight controller only while stick control is on, a gamepad is reporting, the drone is connected and armed, and the flight mode gives the pilot stick authority (for example Stabilize, Alt Hold, Loiter or PosHold). The keyboard does not fly the drone; keys trigger skill-bar actions only. Hotkeys are listed on [Cockpit](/mission-control/cockpit).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Arm">
    Step back from the drone. In the **Cockpit** skill bar, use **Arm** and slide the confirm control to the end (or hold Enter or the bound gamepad button).

    If the pre-flight checklist has open items, the confirm sheet lists them under **Pre-flight checklist incomplete** and needs **Override checklist** before it will arm. The override is recorded as a safety event. If the flight controller refuses to arm, Mission Control shows the reason it gave.

    <Frame caption="Cockpit tab with the drone armed.">
      ![Mission Control Cockpit tab showing an armed drone with the video, HUD and skill bar](/images/mission-control/cockpit-armed.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Take off and hover">
    In **Stabilize**, raise the throttle slowly until the drone lifts off. In **Alt Hold**, the throttle stick centre holds altitude, above centre climbs and below centre descends.

    Hover at 2 to 3 metres. Keep inputs gentle until the drone behaves as you expect. If anything feels wrong, use **Loiter** in the skill bar to hold position (GPS required).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Change flight modes">
    The default skill bar has **Loiter** (`F1`), **Alt Hold** (`F2`) and **Stabilize** (`F3`). **Guided** and **Auto** are in the skill drawer and can be added to an empty slot. The flight controller confirms each change.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Land">
    Fly back over the landing spot and lower the throttle until the drone touches down, or use **Land** (hold to confirm) to descend in place. **Return to Home** (hold to confirm) flies back to the launch point and lands, and needs GPS.

    After touchdown, use the **Arm** slot again if the flight controller has not disarmed itself: while the drone is armed it runs **Disarm**, which you hold to confirm. **Kill Motors** cuts the motors immediately, in the air too, and uses a guarded two-step hold. Use it only in an emergency.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review the flight">
    Open **Logs** in the top navigation for the record of each flight, or the drone's **Logs** tab for that drone's history. See [Flight logs](/mission-control/flight-logs).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Common first-flight problems

- **The drone drifts in one direction.** Recalibrate the compass and accelerometer away from metal, and check the flight controller's mounting direction.
- **The drone oscillates.** Tune it in **Setup** → **PID Tuning**, or use the flight controller's autotune.
- **The sticks do nothing.** Check the **STICK** chip, the arm state and the flight mode; each of them blocks stick input.
- **No video.** See [Video feed](/mission-control/video-feed).
