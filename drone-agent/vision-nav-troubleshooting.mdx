# Troubleshooting

Most vision-nav surprises fall into four families: low flow quality, a blocked pre-arm card, scale errors in `optical_flow_degraded`, and camera-IMU sync drift. Start at the section that matches your symptom. Common questions are answered in the [FAQ](#faq) at the end.

The plugin's own logs are the first stop for anything odd:

```bash
ados plugin logs com.altnautica.vision-nav
```

A configuration the plugin rejects is logged as `vision-nav: config invalid, staying disengaged: ...`.

---

## Low optical-flow quality

Symptom: flow quality on the Vision Nav tab is below 50, the estimator shows `degraded`, or the fallback banner says quality dropped below the safe gate.

### Step 1: light

The Lucas-Kanade tracker follows corners between frames, and both frames need enough light to have corners.

- Add light if you can.
- Use a mono or global-shutter camera if you have one; mono sensors gather more light per pixel.
- An IR illuminator works: the tracker only sees brightness, not colour.

### Step 2: texture

Is the ground featureless: snow, water, fresh asphalt, smooth polished concrete? The tracker needs texture.

- Fly over a more textured surface.
- Lower the flight controller's own flow-quality threshold (`EK3_FLOW_QUAL_MIN` on ArduPilot) only in a safe test area. The EKF still rejects very bad samples at its innovation gate.

### Step 3: yaw rate

The tracker removes rotation using the gyro, but fast yaw moves features out of the 200-corner set between frames.

- Slow the yaw.
- Widen the lens so the same ground stays in view across a yaw step.
- If the camera-IMU sync offset is yellow or red, fix that first (see below); bad sync turns every turn into false translation.

### Step 4: motion blur

Fast horizontal flight with a long exposure smears the corners.

- Shorten the exposure or fly slower.
- A global-shutter camera avoids the direction-dependent smear of a rolling shutter.

## Pre-arm card blocked

The pre-arm card on the Vision Nav tab lists the plugin's checks for the active mode. Each row shows a reason.

| Row | Reason shown | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Companion process active | "Companion has not reported active yet." | Normal until the first good flow sample. Point the camera at textured ground. |
| Companion process active | `Companion state "critical".` | The estimator has been degraded or failed for 2 s or more. Fix flow quality or the scale source; the state returns to active on the next converged sample. |
| Optical flow quality | "Awaiting first optical-flow sample." | No frames are arriving. Check that the agent's vision host is capturing the downward camera. |
| Optical flow quality | "Quality N below gate 50." | See [Low optical-flow quality](#low-optical-flow-quality). |
| Rangefinder healthy | "Rangefinder required in this mode..." | `rangefinder.topology` is `none`. Set it to `fc` or `companion`, or switch to `optical_flow_degraded`. |
| Altitude scale source | "No altitude or depth source healthy." | The degraded mode has no scale source. Check that the flight controller streams `GLOBAL_POSITION_INT` or `VFR_HUD`. |

The `rangefinder` row only checks that a rangefinder is configured. If it is green but the distance on the sensors card is empty or frozen, the reading isn't arriving: check the flight controller's rangefinder setup (topology `fc`, readings older than 200 ms count as missing) or the TF-Luna's serial device and baud.

If the plugin's card is all green and the drone still won't arm, the block is on the flight controller side: EKF origin not set, EKF not using flow, or another arming check. See [Configure optical flow](/drone-agent/vision-nav-optical-flow#before-you-arm).

## Scale errors in `optical_flow_degraded`

Symptom: velocity is consistently off by a factor of 2 to 4, or the estimator sits in `degraded`.

### Step 1: which rung is active?

The sensors card shows `flowScaleSource`:

- **`baro`**: one of the barometer rungs, or the fixed 1.5 m rung (also labelled `baro`). If the estimator is `degraded` while flow quality is fine, you are on the fixed rung.
- **`rangefinder`**: only in `optical_flow`. If you have a working rangefinder, switch to `optical_flow`.
- **empty**: no scale source at all. The pre-arm card blocks.

### Step 2: no barometer messages

The barometer rungs need `GLOBAL_POSITION_INT` or `VFR_HUD` from the flight controller within the last 2 seconds. If both are stale, the ladder drops to the fixed 1.5 m rung. Check the flight controller's stream rates on the MAVLink link to the companion.

### Step 3: barometer drift

Indoor barometers drift. Prop wash on an uncovered barometer drifts further, and HVAC pressure changes can move the reading by a metre or two.

- Cover the flight controller's barometer with open-cell foam.
- Wait about 30 seconds after entering an indoor space before arming so the barometer settles.
- Note that rung 2 uses the first `VFR_HUD` altitude it sees as take-off height. Restart the plugin if it started at a very different altitude from where you take off.

### Step 4: fixed fallback

On the 1.5 m rung the scale is a guess. Flow quality is multiplied by 0.2, so the EKF trusts it very little. Fix the barometer feed, or fit a rangefinder and use `optical_flow`. Don't plan flights around the fixed rung.

## Camera-IMU sync drift

Symptom: the sync offset pill on the sensors card is yellow (10 to 30 ms) or red (above 30 ms), or the fallback banner says time sync drifted beyond 30 ms.

- **No calibration file loaded?** The agent assumes zero offset. A USB camera often has tens of milliseconds of pipeline delay. Install a [calibration](/drone-agent/vision-nav-calibration) with a measured `timeshift_cam_imu`.
- **Calibration loaded but the offset is high?** Something about the camera mode changed (resolution, frame rate, exposure). Recalibrate in the mode you fly.
- **Offset swings from flight to flight?** Check the IMU rate on the sensors card. A low `RAW_IMU` stream rate makes the nearest sample further away from each frame; raise the flight controller's stream rate for raw sensors.

## When in doubt

Switch the flight controller back to its GPS source set (or to a flight mode that doesn't need position), land, and diagnose on the ground. The plugin never changes the flight controller's source set or flight mode for you.

If the diagnosis points at a bug, file an issue on [ADOSExtensions](https://github.com/altnautica/ADOSExtensions/issues) with:

- the mode at the time of the failure,
- the output of `ados plugin logs com.altnautica.vision-nav` covering the failure,
- a screenshot of the Vision Nav tab at the moment it happened,
- the flight controller's log or tlog for the same window.

## FAQ

### Do I need a rangefinder?

For `optical_flow`, yes: it provides the height that turns angular flow into metric velocity. For `optical_flow_degraded`, no: the plugin uses the barometer, at lower accuracy, and drifts above about 5 m.

### Which board do I need?

Any board in the [supported list](/drone-agent/vision-nav-overview#supported-boards). The tracker is light; the plugin is capped at 80 percent of one core and 512 MB RAM.

### Can I fly at night?

Only with light. The tracker needs visible texture. An IR illuminator works because the tracker only uses brightness. The fallback banner appears as soon as quality drops and the estimator degrades, so you see it before the EKF stops trusting the feed.

### How does it compare to GPS?

GPS gives the EKF an absolute position. Optical flow gives it a velocity, and the EKF integrates that into a position relative to where it started, which drifts over time. For most outdoor flights GPS is better. Vision navigation is for where GPS isn't: indoors, under canopy, under bridges, in urban canyons.

### Can I fly outdoors with it?

Yes, and outdoor ground is often easier: grass, gravel, and painted asphalt have plenty of texture. The two outdoor problems are featureless ground (snow, water, fresh asphalt) and fast yaw. Above the rangefinder's range, `optical_flow` loses its height reference.

### What happens if the camera unplugs mid-flight?

The plugin stops emitting flow because no frames arrive. It has no frame-loss timer of its own, so the estimator state on the tab stays at its last value; a flow-rate trend that stops updating is the tell. The flight controller's EKF times out the flow source by its own rules and reports it. Switch to GPS or land. The plugin never triggers RTL or changes flight mode.

### Do I need to recalibrate after every flight?

No. A calibration is per camera, lens, mount, and camera mode. Redo it when one of those changes, or when the sync offset sits in yellow or red during a steady hover.

### How accurate is it?

It depends on the scene, the height, the rangefinder, and the tuning. `optical_flow` with a working rangefinder over textured ground at 1 to 3 m is the best case; `optical_flow_degraded` is noticeably looser. We don't publish per-installation accuracy figures; measure on your own airframe before relying on a number.

### Can I run two instances on one drone?

No. One install per drone runs one estimator, and it owns MAVLink component 198. A second flow source on the same component would collide.

### What about visual-inertial odometry?

It is not part of the current release. The three modes are `off`, `optical_flow`, and `optical_flow_degraded`.
