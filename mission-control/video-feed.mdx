Mission Control plays live H.264 video from a Drone Agent over WebRTC. The agent encodes with the board's hardware encoder when one is present, and falls back to software libx264 otherwise. Encoder and camera settings are per node; see [Video pipeline](/drone-agent/video-pipeline).

## Where video appears

- **Cockpit tab**: full-screen video under the HUD. A drone with several cameras shows stream tabs (keys 1 to 9) and a picture-in-picture inset (key P). See [Cockpit and Skills](/mission-control/cockpit#layout).
- **Overview tab**: a video card for the drone, with the transport switcher.
- **Ground station Overview**: a video card for the drone the ground station receives.
- **Grid view**: a live tile per node, up to four playing at once.
- **Swarm view**: a video rail for the selected hero drone.

## Transports

The video card's transport switcher has four options:

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Auto (best of LAN, P2P)** | The default. Tries LAN Direct, then P2P MQTT. |
| **LAN Direct** | WebRTC (WHEP) straight to the agent on your network. Unavailable with **No agent on LAN**. |
| **P2P MQTT** | WebRTC with the offer and answer relayed through the MQTT broker; media still flows peer to peer. Needs a paired agent; unavailable with **Agent not paired**. |
| **Off** | No video. |

The pill on the card shows the active transport (for example **AUTO · LAN DIRECT**), the link latency, and states such as **CONNECTING…**, **RETRYING IN {n}s** or **AUTO · FAILED**. Your choice persists across sessions.

### Auto cascade

1. **LAN Direct**, with a 10 second timeout. Skipped when Mission Control is served over HTTPS, because the page cannot fetch the agent's plain-HTTP endpoint.
2. **P2P MQTT**, with a 35 second timeout (broker connect, answer and first frame).
3. If both fail, the pill reads **AUTO · FAILED** and the menu shows each transport's error.

There is no cloud video rung in the cascade. On networks that block peer-to-peer (for example symmetric NAT on some cellular carriers), P2P MQTT fails; use a network without symmetric NAT.

### LAN Direct

Mission Control uses the WHEP URL the agent advertises, served on the agent's port 8080 at `/whep` (or `/whep?camera=<id>` for a specific camera), and sends the pairing key with the request. For a drone at `192.168.1.50` the URL is `http://192.168.1.50:8080/whep`.

### Full-rate video while you watch

A drone that shares the WFB radio channel boots on the low-rate `thumbnail` profile (320x180 at 1 fps), so a fleet powering up together does not flood the channel. When you open a detail view of that drone over LAN Direct (the Overview video card, the Cockpit, or a feed on its Agent tab), Mission Control asks the agent for the full-rate `hero` profile with a 30-second lease and renews it every 15 seconds. About 5 seconds after the last of those views closes, it releases the lease and the drone returns to its base profile. A closed or crashed tab simply stops renewing, so the lease lapses on its own. Grid tiles never take a lease, and a ground station is never promoted. The lease call is `POST /api/video/profile`; see [Video pipeline](/drone-agent/video-pipeline).

### ICE

The agent's WebRTC media uses UDP port 8189 on every interface. On the LAN profile it offers host candidates only. The cloud-relay profile adds five STUN servers (`stun.l.google.com`, `stun1.l.google.com` and `stun2.l.google.com` on 19302, `stun.cloudflare.com` and `global.stun.twilio.com` on 3478) and a TCP candidate on 8189. Candidate gathering times out after 2 seconds.

Expected latency per path is on [Video stack](/architecture/video-stack).

## Video card controls

- **Capture screenshot**: saves a still frame.
- **Start recording** / **Stop recording**: records the stream in the browser, with a REC indicator in the frame.
- **Picture in picture**: the browser's floating video window.
- **Enter fullscreen** / **Exit fullscreen**.
- **Reconnect video**.
- **Pop out video**: opens the video in its own window.

The Cockpit's **REC** records video and telemetry together as a flight recording.

## On a ground station screen

For an HDMI screen on a ground station, use the agent's own on-box cockpit at `http://<node>:8080/cockpit/` (add `?kiosk=1` for kiosk mode). See [HDMI kiosk](/ground-agent/hdmi-kiosk).

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The pill shows AGENT VIDEO STOPPED or the video is black">
    On the agent, run `ados diag video` and check that a camera is detected and the video service is running. See [Video pipeline](/drone-agent/video-pipeline).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="LAN Direct is unavailable">
    Mission Control has no reachable agent on the LAN for this drone, or the page is served over HTTPS. Pair the agent by hostname or IP from a page served over plain HTTP, or use the desktop app.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="P2P MQTT fails">
    The agent must be paired to your account and the MQTT broker reachable from both ends. Symmetric NAT blocks the peer-to-peer media path.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="High latency on LAN Direct">
    Check for Wi-Fi congestion and the node's encoder settings. Compare against the figures on [Video stack](/architecture/video-stack).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
