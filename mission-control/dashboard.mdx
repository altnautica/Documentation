The Dashboard (`/`) is the main screen. The Fleet sidebar lists every node on the left. With no node selected, the main area shows one of four fleet views. Select a node and the main area becomes that node's detail panel.

## Top bar

The top navigation has five tabs:

| Tab | Route | What it holds |
|---|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | `/` | Fleet views and node detail (this page) |
| **Plan** | `/plan` | The [mission planner](/mission-control/mission-planning). A dot means the plan has unsaved changes. |
| **Simulate** | `/simulate` | The [3D mission preview](/mission-control/simulation) |
| **Logs** | `/flight-logs` | [Flight logs](/mission-control/flight-logs), reports and replay |
| **MCP** | `/mcp` | The [MCP tab](/mission-control/mcp) |

The right side of the top bar holds, in order:

- The ground-station role (**Direct**, **Relay**, **Receiver** or **Unset**), when Mission Control is talking to a ground station.
- An alert count, when there are unacknowledged alerts.
- A **⌘K** hint for the command palette.
- **Community**: the changelog, with a dot for unseen entries.
- **Join Discord**, **Docs** and **Star on GitHub**.
- **Flash Tool** (the lightning icon), which opens the [Flash Tool](/mission-control/flash-tool).
- **Settings** (the gear), which opens [Settings](/mission-control/settings).
- **Local only** when you are not signed in (click it to sign in), or your account avatar when you are.

In demo mode a **Demo** pill sits next to the logo; its **X** turns demo mode off.

## Command palette

Press Ctrl+K (⌘K on macOS) anywhere. The palette searches:

- **Navigation**: Go to Dashboard, Mission Planner, Simulate, History (the Logs tab), MCP or Config, and **Open Cockpit** for a selected drone.
- **Commands**: **Connect Drone**, **Return to Home All** and **Check Battery Status**.
- **Flight**: the selected drone's flight and safety Skills, such as Arm, Take Off, Land, Return to Home, Pause, Abort, Kill Motors and mode presets. They use the same confirmations as the [cockpit](/mission-control/cockpit#skill-bar-and-confirmations).
- **Parameters**: type two or more characters while connected to jump to a parameter in the Parameters tab.

Esc closes the palette. Flight hotkeys are on [Cockpit and Skills](/mission-control/cockpit#default-hotkeys).

## Fleet sidebar

The sidebar header reads **Fleet**, with **+** (**Add drone**) to open the [Connect to Drone](/mission-control/connecting) dialog and a chevron to collapse the panel. **Fleet Overview** clears the selection and returns to the fleet views. A search box filters by name or device id, and the footer counts nodes.

Each row shows:

- A type tile (Drone, Flight controller, Ground station or Workstation) whose ring shows health: steady when live, pulsing when stale, dimmed when offline. A small Wi-Fi mark means the node is on your LAN.
- The node name and a **…** actions menu (also on right-click).
- Up to three badges, for example the firmware and airframe (such as ArduPilot copter), **FC + SBC** or **FC**, **Relayed**, a ground-station role, or **Stale** and **Offline**.

## Fleet views

With no node selected, a switcher picks the view. Grid is the default.

### Grid

The **Agent Overview** grid has one tile per node with live video where the node has it.

- **Stat tiles**: Total, Live, Stale and Offline, plus Video, FC and GPU nodes when they apply.
- **Filter chips**: All, Live, Video, Stale/offline, and Workstation when workstations exist.
- **Tiles**: chips for liveness, node type, **FC ON** or **FC OFF**, and **ARMED** or **DISARMED**. Drone tiles show Battery, GPS, Alt, Mode, CPU, Mem, Temp and Video. Ground tiles show the role, link state and RSSI, SNR, Loss and Link. Workstation tiles show CPU, Mem, Disk and Temp.

Up to four video feeds play at once. Pin a tile to keep its feed, or pause it.

### Overview

A map beside four cards: **Fleet Status**, **Active Missions**, **Fleet Battery** and **Alerts**, followed by fleet telemetry, **Recent Alerts** and **Quick Actions**.

### Nodes and Swarm

**Nodes** is one console for the whole fleet: how each node is reached, relay streams, and per-node, bulk and fleet actions. **Swarm** shows one row per fleet slot from the swarm-bus beacons, with formation and bulk flight actions. Both are described on [Nodes and Swarm views](/mission-control/nodes-fleet-view).

## Node detail

Select a node to open its detail panel. The header shows the node name, connection and authority chips, the device id, **Delete**, link and traffic indicators, and for a connected drone, **Open cockpit** (the monitor icon) and **Reboot FC**. Close it with **X** to return to the fleet views.

### Agent Status card

The **Agent Status** card on a drone or ground station Overview shows CPU, memory, disk and temperature, the age of the last MAVLink heartbeat, and the control-plane round-trip time to the agent in milliseconds (hover title **Control-plane round-trip time to the agent**).

| Round-trip time | Colour |
|---|---|
| Under 80 ms | Green |
| 80 ms to under 250 ms | Amber |
| 250 ms or more | Red |

The round-trip time appears only while the node is live over a direct LAN link. Over the cloud relay, or before the first measurement, it is not shown. A node that stops answering shows **Agent offline**, and old data is marked **Stale data** with its age.

## First run

On first launch a setup wizard asks for your language, shows the **Before You Fly** disclaimer (you must accept it to continue), then sets jurisdiction, units, demo mode, audio alerts, map position, operating region and theme. Re-run it from **Settings → General → Re-run Setup Wizard**.

Until you sign in, a banner notes that your data is saved in this browser only.
