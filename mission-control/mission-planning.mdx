The **Plan** tab is the mission planner. You work on one flight plan at a time: open or create a plan in the library, place waypoints and actions on the map, review the mission, then send it to the vehicle.

<Frame caption="Plan tab with a demo plan open: planner bar on top, plan outline on the left, tool rail on the map, inspector on the right and the review dock along the bottom.">
  ![Plan tab showing a waypoint mission on the map with the outline, inspector and review dock](/images/mission-control/plan-mission.png)
</Frame>

<Warning>
Uploading a mission changes what the aircraft flies in AUTO. Read [Safety](/operations/safety) before you fly a new mission.
</Warning>

## Layout

With no plan open, the **FLIGHT PLANS** library sits at the left of a full-size map. It is the way in: search, folders, **New Plan**, **Import** and **From Drone**. Opening or creating a plan hides it and brings up the planner.

| Area | What it holds |
|---|---|
| Planner bar (top) | **Plans** (opens the library over the map, also `Ctrl+O`), the plan name (click to rename), a dot when there are unsaved changes, **Vehicle**, an issues chip, **Save**, **Send to vehicle** and the **⋯** menu |
| Plan outline (left) | **Mission** (one row per waypoint), **Shapes** (drawn polygons and circles), **Geofence**, **Rally points**, **Points of interest**. `[` shows or hides it |
| Tool rail (on the map) | Drawing tools, the pattern gallery, undo and redo, map layers, offline area download and shortcut help |
| Inspector (right) | Settings for whatever is selected. `]` shows or hides it; `\` hides or shows both side panels |
| Dock (bottom of the map) | A stats strip (waypoints, distance, estimated time, maximum altitude, average speed) and the review tabs **Altitude**, **Terrain**, **Issues**, **Energy**, **Site** and **Checklist** |

The map opens where you last left it. On first use it centres on your GPS position when location is turned on, and otherwise shows the whole world. Opening a plan fits the map to its waypoints.

### Outline

Click a row to select it; `Ctrl`-click adds to the selection and `Shift`-click selects a range. Drag a waypoint row to reorder it. Hover between two rows to insert a waypoint there, or hover a row to delete it. Clicking a group header opens that group's settings in the inspector, and the **+** on a header adds an item or arms the matching map tool. Selecting a waypoint on the map scrolls its row into view.

### Inspector

The inspector follows the selection:

| Selection | Inspector shows |
|---|---|
| Nothing | **Mission**: **Defaults**, **Start from a template** (only while the plan is empty) and **Plan elements** rows that open the pattern, geofence, rally point, point of interest and transform settings |
| One waypoint | **Waypoint**: command, position, altitude, speed, per-command parameters and attached actions |
| Two or more waypoints | **Batch** edit |
| A pattern, geofence, rally point, point of interest or transform | That item's settings, with a back arrow to **Mission** |

Drag a number field's label or unit left or right to change its value (a field without either shows a small drag handle); hold `Shift` for steps ten times larger or `Alt` for a tenth. The arrow keys step the value too.

### Planner bar menu

The **⋯** menu holds **Download from drone**, **Import boundary (KML/SHP)**, every export and the **Flight brief (PDF)**, **Copy share link**, **Save as new plan**, **Reverse waypoints**, **Transform mission…**, **Simulate in 3D** and **Discard plan**. Every menu item, panel toggle and dock tab is also a command in the command palette (`Ctrl+K`).

## Create a mission

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open or create a plan">
    Click **Plan** in the top navigation. Pick a plan in the **FLIGHT PLANS** library, or click **New Plan**. While a plan is open, **Plans** in the planner bar brings the library back.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the defaults">
    With nothing selected, the inspector shows **Mission**. In **Defaults**, set the altitude, speed, accept radius and altitude frame: **REL** (relative to home), **MSL** (above mean sea level) or **Terrain** (follows the terrain). New waypoints take these values.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place waypoints">
    Choose the Waypoint tool (`W`) and click the map. Use the Takeoff (`K`), Land (`J`), Loiter (`O`) and ROI (`R`) tools for those commands. Click a waypoint on the map or in the outline to edit its command, altitude, speed and attached actions in the inspector.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review">
    Open the **Issues** tab in the dock (`I`, or click the issues chip in the planner bar). Issues update as you edit. Check the **Terrain** and **Altitude** tabs (`T`) for ground clearance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and send">
    Click **Save** to store the plan in the library, then **Send to vehicle**. The button label shows the state: **Fix n issues** while blocking issues remain (clicking it opens the **Issues** tab, or the dialog when a fence or rally points can still be sent), then **Sending…** and **Sent ✓**. A chip next to it shows when the vehicle holds an older mission than the editor.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Send to vehicle

**Send to vehicle** opens a dialog listing what the plan holds: the mission, the geofence and the rally points, each shown only when present. The button is available when the plan holds waypoints, a geofence or rally points, so a fence or rally points can be sent on their own. The dialog names the vehicle that receives the upload; when the plan is assigned to a different vehicle, it says so and offers a button to switch to that vehicle. Blocking issues stop the mission from being sent, with a **Review issues** link to the dock. The dialog also shows pre-flight checklist progress with an **Open checklist** link; the checklist is informational and does not block sending. **Send** sends the selected items in order and reports each one as sent or failed. A failure does not stop the remaining items.


## Map tools

| Key | Tool | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `V` | Select | Select and drag waypoints |
| `W` | Waypoint | Add a navigation waypoint |
| `K` | Takeoff | Add a takeoff point |
| `J` | Land | Add a landing point |
| `O` | Loiter | Add a loiter point |
| `R` | Region of interest | Add an ROI |
| `Y` | Rally point | Add a rally point |
| `B` | Point of Interest | Add a point of interest |
| `D` | Set datum | Set the altitude datum |
| `P` | Polygon | Draw a polygon (survey area, fence, boundary) |
| `C` | Circle | Draw a circle |
| `M` | Measure | Measure distance and bearing |

On the tool rail, the waypoint tools (`W` `K` `J` `O` `R`), the draw tools (`P` `C`) and the marker tools (`Y` rally point, `B` point of interest, `D` datum) are each grouped behind one button that shows the last tool used; its arrow opens the rest. The **Patterns** button opens a gallery of the pattern types and mission templates. To clear the whole plan, use **⋯ › Discard plan**.

Right-click the map for the context menu, for example to insert a waypoint at that point.

## Commands

A waypoint's own command is always a navigation command. Actions attach to a waypoint through its action timeline.

**Navigation commands**

| Label | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Waypoint | Fly to the point |
| Spline Waypoint | Fly a smooth curve through the point |
| Takeoff | Climb to the waypoint altitude |
| Land | Land at the point |
| Loiter (unlimited) | Hold at the point until the mission is advanced |
| Loiter (Time) | Hold for a set time |
| Loiter (Turns) | Circle a set number of turns |
| Return to Launch | Fly home |
| VTOL Takeoff | Vertical takeoff (VTOL aircraft) |
| VTOL Land | Vertical landing (VTOL aircraft) |
| Payload Place | Descend and release a payload |
| Land Start | Mark the start of a landing sequence |

**Attached actions**

| Group | Actions |
|---|---|
| Camera | `DO_SET_CAM_TRIGG`, `DO_DIGICAM`, `DO_MOUNT_CONTROL`, `ROI`, `DO_SET_ROI_NONE` |
| Movement | `DO_SET_SPEED`, `CONDITION_YAW` |
| Payload | `DO_SET_SERVO`, `DO_GRIPPER`, `DO_WINCH`, `DO_AUX_FUNCTION` |
| Flow | `DO_JUMP`, `DELAY`, `CONDITION_DISTANCE` |
| System | `DO_FENCE_ENABLE`, `DO_SET_HOME` |

<Note>
iNav flies only three attached actions: `ROI`, `DO_JUMP` and `CONDITION_YAW`. Upload to an iNav flight controller refuses any other action. See [iNav guide](/mission-control/inav-guide).
</Note>

## Terrain profile

With the **Terrain Following** frame, or to check clearance on any plan, the planner looks up ground elevation for each waypoint from the Open Elevation API and caches the results. The dock's **Terrain** tab plots ground elevation against the flight path and marks legs with low clearance. Validation reports a blocking issue when clearance is too low, and an advisory when terrain could not be checked.

## Batch edit

Select several waypoints (`Ctrl`-click or `Shift`-click, on the map or in the outline). The inspector switches to **Batch** edit with four operations: set altitude, set speed, set command, and delete the selected waypoints.

## Transform mission

**Transform mission…** in the **⋯** menu opens the transform settings in the inspector. It moves, rotates or scales the whole mission around its centre. Relative positions and altitudes stay the same.

## Mission validation

Validation runs continuously and is listed in the dock's **Issues** tab; clicking an issue selects its waypoint. Blocking issues stop the mission from being sent; advisories are shown but do not.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Blocking issues">
    - Empty mission
    - Mission starts above the ground with no Takeoff command
    - An action used as a navigation command, or an action with no location
    - Invalid coordinates (waypoints, actions or rally points)
    - Duplicate sequence numbers
    - Altitude above the ceiling or below the minimum
    - Waypoint outside the geofence or inclusion zone, or inside an exclusion zone
    - A leg that crosses an exclusion zone
    - Invalid `DO_JUMP` target, or a jump loop with no exit
    - Terrain clearance too low
    - RTL altitude too low
    - Rally point outside the fence or inside an exclusion zone
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Advisories">
    - Too few waypoints, first waypoint is not Takeoff, home not set
    - No Land or Return to Launch at the end
    - Duplicate waypoints and excessive leg distance
    - Leg or waypoint beyond the link range
    - Turn radius too tight for the aircraft
    - `DO_JUMP` that repeats forever or targets an unreachable item
    - Altitude or terrain not checked
    - Self-intersecting fence, rally point above the fence ceiling
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

The dock's **Energy** tab gives a rough energy, flight-time and battery-swap figure from a default power draw. Check it against your own power curve.

## Mission templates

Templates are in the **Templates** tab of the rail's pattern gallery, and in **Start from a template** in the **Mission** inspector while the plan is empty. A template builds a ready-to-fly plan from the current map view, for example **Grid survey**, **Property mapping**, **Orbit inspection** and **Corridor scan**. A template uses your drawn boundary, or a box around the map centre when there is none, and asks before replacing the current plan.

## Map aids

- **Place search** (`/`) centres the map on a place.
- **Map layers** (`L`) shows KML and raster (GeoTIFF) overlays. See [File formats](/mission-control/file-formats).
- **Download area for offline** on the tool rail saves map tiles for the visible area. Tiles you view are also kept automatically. See [Data](/mission-control/settings#data).
- The basemap control switches between satellite, street, topographic and other maps, including **FIELD** when a paired ground station serves a tile pack.
- The dock's **Site** tab shows sunrise, sunset, golden hour and weather at the first waypoint.

## Import, export and download

| Action | Where |
|---|---|
| **Send to vehicle** | Planner bar. Sends the mission over the MAVLink mission protocol (MSP on iNav), plus the geofence and rally points when present |
| **Download from drone** | **⋯** menu, or **From Drone** in the library. Reads the mission stored on the flight controller |
| **Import boundary (KML/SHP)** | **⋯** menu |
| Export | **⋯** menu: `.altmission`, `.waypoints`, `.plan` (QGC), KML, `.kmz`, CSV, and a **Flight brief (PDF)** |
| **Copy share link** | **⋯** menu. Encodes the plan in a link; large plans need file export |
| **Import** | Library. Loads a plan file |

See [File formats](/mission-control/file-formats) for each format.

## Keyboard shortcuts

`Ctrl` is `⌘` on macOS. Tool keys are in [Map tools](#map-tools). The panel and dock keys (`T`, `G`, `I`, `L`, `[`, `]`, `\`) work only while a plan is open, and no planner shortcut fires while a dialog is open.

| Shortcut | Action |
|---|---|
| `T` | Show or hide the dock on the **Altitude** tab |
| `G` | Show or hide the pattern settings |
| `I` | Show or hide the dock on the **Issues** tab |
| `[` | Show or hide the plan outline |
| `]` | Show or hide the inspector |
| `\` | Show or hide both side panels |
| `/` | Search for a place |
| `L` | Show or hide map layers |
| `Ctrl+Z` / `Ctrl+Shift+Z` or `Ctrl+Y` | Undo / redo |
| `Ctrl+C` / `Ctrl+V` | Copy / paste the selected waypoints |
| Arrow keys | Nudge the selected waypoints |
| `Del` | Delete the selected waypoints (one undo step) |
| `Ctrl`-click / `Shift`-click | Add to the selection / select a range |
| `Esc` | Close the plan library sheet, then cancel drawing, then clear the selection, then return the inspector to **Mission**. An open menu or popover closes first |
| `Ctrl+S` / `Ctrl+Shift+S` | Save plan / save plan as |
| `Ctrl+O` | Open the plan library |
| `Ctrl+N` | New plan (desktop app only) |
