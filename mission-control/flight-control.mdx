The **Flight** tab is a drone's live telemetry surface: instruments and actions on the left, a full map on the right. It has no video; video, the HUD and the Skill Bar live on the [Cockpit](/mission-control/cockpit) tab.

<Warning>
The action panel and the map menu command a real aircraft. Read [Safety](/operations/safety) before you arm.
</Warning>

<Frame caption="A drone's Flight tab with the action panel and map.">

## Layout

Select a drone and open **Flight**. The left column holds, top to bottom:

- **HUD**: artificial horizon with pitch ladder and roll arc, speed and altitude tapes, heading, battery, GPS, mode, armed state, signal and flight timer. You can detach it to a separate window.
- **Telemetry readout**: ALT, SPD, HDG and VS, with satellites, battery, mode and a **link silent** warning when telemetry stops. Its controls open the [telemetry deck](/mission-control/telemetry#telemetry-deck).
- **Action panel** (below).
- **Health and info**: the sensor health grid, health percentage, voltage, satellites and fix type, then vehicle, identity and statistics.
- **RC input**, when the drone has no ExpressLRS CRSF lane, and the FC status text feed.

The map on the right shows the drone, its path, home and the active mission. The handle on its left edge hides or shows the telemetry column.

## Action panel

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Loadout** | Picks the battery and equipment fitted for this flight. It is frozen into the flight record when you arm. |
| **Pre-Flight Check {n}/18** | Opens the pre-flight checklist (hardware, software, environment and mission items). It turns green when the checklist is ready. |
| **ARM** / **DISARM** | Arms with a slide confirmation or disarms with a hold. Disabled with **No flight-controller link** while the arm state is unknown. |
| Mode select | Switches flight mode. The list comes from the connected firmware. |
| Pause | Pauses a mission in Auto, resumes a paused mission, or holds position. Shown on vehicles with autonomous navigation. |
| RTL | Return to home. |
| Takeoff altitude | 1 to 120 m, default 10 m. Shared with takeoff fired from a hotkey or gamepad button. |
| Takeoff | Arms and climbs to the takeoff altitude. |
| Land | Lands at the current position. |
| Abort | Commands an immediate land. Always shown. |
| Kill | Cuts all motors after a guarded hold. Always shown. Emergency only. |
| **Follow Me** | Shown when armed on a vehicle with autonomous navigation. The drone follows this device's GPS position. |

Every button is a Skill with the same confirmation, hotkey and stale-link rules as the cockpit. See [Skill bar and confirmations](/mission-control/cockpit#skill-bar-and-confirmations) and [Default hotkeys](/mission-control/cockpit#default-hotkeys). Hotkeys and gamepad buttons work on the Flight tab too, but stick control streams only from the Cockpit tab.

## Map actions

Right-click the map for the context menu.

- **Fly Here** (shortcut G), **Fly Here at Alt...**, **Loiter Here** and **Land Here**: shown when the drone is connected, armed and in Guided, Auto, Guided NoGPS or Loiter. PX4 copters also get **Orbit Here...**.
- **Point Camera Here**, **Clear Camera ROI**, **Trigger Camera**, **Set Home Here**, **Set EKF Origin** and **Add Rally Point** when connected.
- **Add POI Marker**, **Copy Coordinates** and **Measure from Drone**.

Fly Here opens a confirmation with the target's latitude, longitude, distance and ETA, and an altitude field (2 to 120 m, starting at the current relative altitude). Hold **Hold to Confirm** for 1.5 s to send it.

## Mission progress

During a mission the map shows the mission path and progress, with an overlay to pause or resume. Plan missions on the **Plan** tab; see [Mission planning](/mission-control/mission-planning).

## Extensions

Extension surfaces installed on a drone appear as extra node tabs. Install extensions from the drone's **Agent → Extensions** page; see [Install extensions from Mission Control](/mission-control/installing-plugins).
