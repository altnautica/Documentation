A ground station can carry three optional front-panel parts. Each one works on its own, and the node runs normally without any of them.

| Part | What it does | Service |
|---|---|---|
| I2C OLED (128x64 or 128x32) | A four-line status frame. View only. | `ados-oled-i2c` |
| Four buttons | Navigate the touch LCD. | `ados-pic` (the PIC arbiter reads and decodes the presses and passes them to the LCD renderer) |
| SPI touch LCD (480x320) | The full on-device interface: dashboard, detail pages, settings, mesh role, pairing. | `ados-oled` (runs the native `ados-display` renderer) |

The OLED and the LCD (or an HDMI monitor) can be fitted at the same time.

## OLED status display

The I2C OLED shows one status frame, refreshed every 2 seconds from the agent's ground-station status:

1. The hostname.
2. The LAN IP address.
3. The link: `link: idle` before a drone is heard, then `link <state> <rssi>dBm`.
4. The status: `PAIRING <n>s` while a mesh accept window is open, otherwise `role: <role>`, then `uplink up` or `ready`.

<Frame caption="The I2C OLED status frame on a direct-role ground station with a linked drone.">
  ![I2C OLED showing four lines: hostname, LAN IP, link connected with RSSI in dBm, and role direct](/images/ground-agent/oled-status.png)
</Frame>

The OLED has no menu and does not read the buttons.

### Wiring and detection

Wire SDA, SCL, VCC (3.3 V) and GND to the board's I2C bus 1. On a Raspberry Pi the installer enables I2C; reboot once after the install so the bus appears. The service starts only when `/dev/i2c-1` exists, then probes address `0x3C` and then `0x3D`. With no panel answering, it exits cleanly.

### Settings

Set these in `/etc/ados/env` and run `sudo systemctl restart ados-oled-i2c`:

| Variable | Effect |
|---|---|
| `ADOS_OLED_ADDR` | Use exactly this I2C address (for example `0x3D`) instead of probing. |
| `ADOS_OLED_BUS` | I2C bus number. Default `1`. |
| `ADOS_OLED_CONTROLLER` | `sh1106` for an SH1106 panel. Default SSD1306. |
| `ADOS_OLED_HEIGHT` | `32` for a 128x32 panel. Default 64. |

Brightness is `ground_station.ui.oled.brightness` (0 to 255, default 204), applied as the panel contrast whenever it changes. Set it with `PUT /api/v1/ground-station/ui/oled`.

## Buttons

Four momentary buttons drive the touch LCD. A press is short or long: a long press is held at least 2 seconds and fires on release; holding 6 seconds or more cancels the press.

| Button | BCM GPIO | Header pin | Short press | Long press |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | GPIO 5 | Pin 29 | Next tab | Toggle backlight |
| B2 | GPIO 6 | Pin 31 | Uplink detail | Pair drone page (with QR) |
| B3 | GPIO 13 | Pin 33 | Confirm | Pair drone page |
| B4 | GPIO 19 | Pin 35 | Back | More menu |

These are the fallback pins. A board profile can declare its own pins in a `buttons` block (`up`, `down`, `select`, `back`), which the installer writes into the board record at `/run/ados/board.json`; the four pins map in that order to B1 to B4. The Raspberry Pi 3 and 4B profiles use the pins above.

### Remap the buttons

Remaps are stored in `ground_station.ui.buttons.mapping` and written from Mission Control (node panel, **Buttons**) or with `PUT /api/v1/ground-station/ui/buttons`. The keys are `B1_short`, `B1_long` through `B4_long`. The panel acts on these eight actions:

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| `cycle_screen` | Next tab |
| `back` | Close a detail page, or return to the dashboard |
| `menu` | Open the More menu (or Settings) |
| `confirm` | Confirm the focused control |
| `toggle_backlight` | Toggle the LCD backlight |
| `show_network` | Open the Uplink detail page |
| `show_qr` | Open the Pair drone page |
| `pair_drone` | Open the Pair drone page |

Any other action name does nothing.

## Touch display (SPI LCD)

The LCD is drawn by the native `ados-display` renderer under the `ados-oled` unit.

### Auto-provisioning

<Steps>
  <Step title="Detect">
    The board's hardware profile lists its supported displays. With `--display auto` (the ground-station default) the installer picks the panel the board declares; `--display <id>` selects one explicitly and `--display none` opts out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Provision">
    The installer enables the display overlay and the kernel modules, and records the framebuffer, rotation and panel in `/etc/ados/display.conf`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bind">
    After the reboot the renderer finds the framebuffer and the touch input attaches. If the panel never binds, the installer's probe restores the previous boot configuration. If the touch controller is missing when the renderer starts, or drops out later, the renderer keeps looking for it every 500 ms, so a late-loading driver or a re-seated panel gets touch back without a restart.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Pages

A bottom tab bar holds five tabs: **Dashboard**, **Video**, **Settings**, **Link stats** and **Channel hops**. The top bar shows the hostname, the role, CPU, RAM and temperature, and the clock.

The dashboard has four tiles. Tap one to open its detail page:

- **Radio link**: signal, rate and FEC counters. Shows **Hardware** while no radio adapter is found.
- **Drone**: the paired drone. Shows **Pair drone** while no drone has been heard.
- **Mesh**: role, peers and gateway. Shows **Setup wizard** while setup is not finished or the role is unset, and **MESH N/A** on a direct node that cannot join a mesh.
- **Uplink / Cloud**: the active uplink and cloud reachability.

Other detail pages: **Pair drone** (paired drone, key fingerprint, two-tap **Unpair**), **Access point** (name, passphrase and join QR code), **Diagnostics**, **About** and **Calibration**. The **More** menu holds the overflow entries, and an installed extension can add its own page.

### Mesh detail page

Tap the **Mesh** tile to open the mesh detail page. It shows the active role, then one of three bodies: a notice on a direct node, a "mesh down" notice while the mesh forms, or the list of peers. The footer shows the selected gateway and a partition badge.

<Frame caption="The Mesh detail page on a receiver: role, peer list and gateway footer.">
  ![Ground station LCD Mesh detail page showing the receiver role, a list of mesh peers and the selected gateway](/images/ground-agent/display/mesh-detail.png)
</Frame>

To change the role, tap **Switch role**. A picker opens with **DIRECT**, **RELAY** and **RECEIVER**. A role change re-forms the link, so it takes two taps: the first arms the choice ("Tap again: RELAY"), the second writes it through `PUT /api/v1/ground-station/role`. Mesh pairing itself runs from Mission Control or REST; see [Build a mesh](/ground-agent/mesh-setup#mesh-pairing).

  ![Ground station LCD Mesh page with the role picker open and the relay row reading Tap again: RELAY](/images/ground-agent/display/mesh-role-picker.png)
</Frame>

### Touch gestures

| Gesture | Threshold | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Tap | Under 400 ms, under 12 px of movement | Open a tile or press a control |
| Long press | 400 ms or more, under 12 px | Press-and-hold controls |
| Swipe | Under 250 ms, 24 px or more | Move between pages |
| Drag | Any other motion | Scroll a list |

### Calibration

Touch calibration is a nine-point (3x3) capture. It runs from the setup wizard's **Local display** step, from Mission Control (node panel, **Display**), or from the **Calibration** page on the panel. Results persist across reboots.

### Supported boards

| Board | Panel | Controller | Touch | Resolution |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Raspberry Pi 4B | Waveshare 3.5" RPi LCD (A) | ILI9486 | ADS7846 | 480x320 |
| Raspberry Pi 3 | Waveshare 3.5" RPi LCD (A) | ILI9486 | ADS7846 | 480x320 |
| Radxa Cubie A7Z | Waveshare 3.5" RPi LCD (A) | ILI9486 | ADS7846 | 480x320 |
| Radxa ROCK 5C Lite | Waveshare 3.5" RPi LCD (A) | ILI9486 | ADS7846 | 480x320 |

The Raspberry Pi 4B and the ROCK 5C Lite also support an alternative wiring: an 800x480 HDMI panel with an XPT2046 resistive touch layer on SPI. A rig uses one wiring or the other. The touch layer is calibrated with the same nine-point capture.

A new board adds a `displays` block to its hardware profile; see [HAL and tiers](/drone-agent/hal-and-tiers).

## Mission Control mirror

The **Display** tab of the ground station's node panel in Mission Control mirrors the panel: type, controller, resolution, rotation, last-touch age, the current page and the calibration state, with a live page preview. You can switch pages and start calibration from there. The **Buttons** tab holds the button mapping and Bluetooth gamepad pairing. See [Ground station node panel](/mission-control/ground-station-panel).

## Graceful degradation

Every part of the front panel is optional. With no OLED, its service exits cleanly. With no LCD, the renderer does not start. The dashboard, the cockpit and Mission Control still expose every setting.
