The Ground Agent has no internal battery. Power it over USB-C from a wall adapter, a USB-C PD power bank, a vehicle adapter, or another regulated field source, and pick the runtime each job needs.

## Recommended power sources

| Source | Use it for | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| USB-C wall adapter | Bench work, lab testing, fixed setups | Use a supply that sustains the board's required output under load. |
| USB-C PD power bank | Field operation | The best portable option. Pick a bank rated for sustained output, not only peak. |
| Vehicle or field battery adapter | Long sessions | Regulated USB-C output only. No bare battery leads into the board. |
| Laptop USB-C power | Short setup only | Many laptops limit current. Use separate power when video, the Wi-Fi AP, HDMI or extra radios are active. |

<Warning>
Do not judge a power bank by its mAh label. Most banks print mAh at the internal cell voltage, not at the USB output. Estimate runtime in watt-hours.
</Warning>

## Planning draw

These figures are planning estimates, not measurements of a specific build.

| Ground Agent mode | Planning draw | Covers |
|---|---:|---|
| Direct, headless node | ~7.5 W | One radio, no HDMI kiosk, normal local clients |
| Kiosk or dual-radio node | ~9-11 W | HDMI kiosk, a second radio, or heavier uplink use |
| Supply ceiling | 15 W | Supply sizing and brownout margin, not average runtime |

The ceiling matters because a weak supply can reset USB radios during load spikes. Use it for a worst-case runtime estimate only.

## Runtime estimates

These planning estimates assume 75-85% usable energy after USB conversion losses.

| Power bank label | Usable energy | ~7.5 W direct | ~10 W kiosk or dual-radio | 15 W ceiling |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 10,000 mAh | ~28-31 Wh | ~3.7-4.1 h | ~2.8-3.1 h | ~1.9-2.1 h |
| 20,000 mAh | ~56-63 Wh | ~7.5-8.4 h | ~5.6-6.3 h | ~3.7-4.2 h |
| 30,000 mAh | ~83-94 Wh | ~11.1-12.5 h | ~8.3-9.4 h | ~5.5-6.3 h |
| 99 Wh PD bank | ~74-84 Wh | ~9.9-11.2 h | ~7.4-8.4 h | ~5.0-5.6 h |

Old banks, thin cables, heat, and other devices on the same bank all reduce runtime.

## USB tether and power

USB tether is a data path first. A laptop can back-power the board, but that is not a field setup.

- Power the Ground Agent from a wall adapter or a USB-C power bank.
- Use a separate USB-C cable that is explicitly data-capable for the tether.
- Treat laptop back-power as a short setup convenience.
- If video freezes or the USB network drops under load, retest with a known-good power source before changing radio settings.

## Brownout symptoms

Power problems often look like radio or network problems.

| Symptom | Likely cause | First fix |
|---|---|---|
| USB tether appears and disappears | Host power limit or a bad cable | Separate power and a known data cable |
| Video freezes when more clients join | Supply sag under Wi-Fi load | A stronger USB-C source |
| Radio adapter disappears from diagnostics | Brownout on the USB bus | Change the power source and cable |
| HDMI kiosk works, then drops video | Load spike from the display and browser | A higher-output USB-C source |
| Works on wall power but not on a power bank | The bank cannot sustain output | A PD bank with a sustained output rating |

## Field checklist

- Use a USB-C source that sustains the required output.
- Carry one known-good data cable and one known-good power cable.
- Keep the power bank out of direct sun.
- Start long sessions with the bank fully charged.
- For USB tether, keep power and data on separate cables whenever the hardware allows it.
